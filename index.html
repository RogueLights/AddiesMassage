How I create a massage that is safe, unhurried and made just for you

  • Experience with bodies that need extra care

    Before massage school I spent years as a caregiver in group homes, memory care, and assisted living. I am comfortable with wheelchairs, walkers, transfers, medical equipment, limited range of motion, and fragile skin.

  • You set the pace

    We talk first about what hurts, what feels good, and what to avoid. You can stay clothed, stay in your chair, or lie on the table. You can stop or change anything at any time.

  • Gentle relaxation or focused pain relief

    Some sessions are quiet and restful. Others use neuromuscular therapy and coached movement to get at the root of long-standing pain. Most people want a mix, and that is fine.

  • I can come to you

    Mobile sessions at your home or care facility are built for clients who find travel hard. I bring everything, including a massage chair for people who cannot use a table.

About Addie

As a child, I pretended to run a toothbrushing shop for seniors who couldn't brush their own teeth anymore. I've always wanted to be part of someone's story and a comfort on their worst days.

At 18 I started working as an aide in group homes, memory care, and assisted living, and later became a nurse. Along the way, one thing struck me in nearly every case: the people I cared for were touched all day, to be showered, helped to walk, and assessed, but almost never just to help them feel good, calm, or acknowledged. As a nurse, what I usually had to offer someone in pain wasn't time or touch. It was a pill.

In 2019, while recovering from major abdominal surgery, my mom massaged my hands and my dad massaged my feet between doses of pain medication. For that moment, my body stopped being a problem to solve and was simply allowed to exist. I thought of my patients, who might never get to feel that.

I graduated from East West College in Portland in 2022, learned targeted deep tissue work from chiropractors and experienced LMTs, and completed a course in neuromuscular therapy. Today I offer mobile massage tailored to clients with mobility challenges, plus sessions in my peaceful garden studio. Some sessions are quiet and restful. Others use verbal feedback and coached muscle contractions to get to the root of your pain.

Accessibility

In person

  • Mobile massage brings the session to you, so you never have to travel.
  • Chair massage and in-wheelchair massage for anyone who cannot use a table.
  • Help with transfers and positioning from someone with years of caregiving experience.
  • Clothing stays on if you prefer. Draping and pressure are always your choice.
  • Extra time is built into every first visit so nothing is rushed.
  • Caregivers, family, and service animals are welcome.
  • Ask me about parking, steps, and bathroom access at the studio before your first visit. I am happy to describe everything in detail or send photos.

The studio

My studio is in my sweet, blue backyard cottage with its own restroom. The space is only used for my practice so it's quiet and private; though occasionally a cat or house sparrow will peek in. You will have the option for a chair massage or a more traditional massage atop a heated table. I invite you to enjoy my happy place!

Exact address will be shared upon booking confirmation. Please note the studio is not currently fully ADA accessible – contact me to see if we can safely accommodate you here.
